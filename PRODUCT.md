# Product

Resilient Gateway for ePay Paycenter connects WooCommerce to ePay and optionally
rechecks missing payment responses. Shop staff reconcile exceptions while site
administrators maintain the connection.

The administration interface belongs to WooCommerce: native order navigation,
tables, controls and order panels, with English and Greek copy. Reviewing a case
records a staff acknowledgement only; it never changes payment, stock or order
state. Unknown bank outcomes must remain explicit. Bulk acknowledgement is for
unconfirmed and historical cases only; discrepancies and technical problems
require individual review.

Order references must remain available across retries. Bank links may use only
recorded identifiers with the appropriate provenance and known payment method.
The interface must not imply that opening AdminTool searches a reference unless
that behavior is supported.
